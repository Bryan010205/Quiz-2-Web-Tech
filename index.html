Newsly Is Live: Clean, Smart News—Without the Noise

Today, we’re excited to officially launch Newsly—a news app built for people who want clarity, not chaos. Over the past few years, many of us have felt overwhelmed by endless headlines, opinionated takes, and notifications that pull us in a dozen directions. We created Newsly to do the opposite: help you catch up fast, stay informed, and move on with your day.

What makes Newsly different? First, Newsly curates trustworthy stories from verified sources, prioritizing context over clickbait. Our Morning Brief gives you a concise, five-minute snapshot of what matters, while Real-Time Alerts keep you updated only when something truly important breaks. Prefer to listen on the go? Switch on Listen Mode and the app will read the news aloud—perfect for commutes, workouts, or chores.

Second, Newsly respects your time and attention. The interface is minimal by design: clean typography, readable layouts, and both Light and Dark Mode for comfort day or night. You won’t see noisy pop-ups or distracting clutter—just information clearly presented.

Third, Newsly is built for everyone. With a built-in Language Switch, you can browse headlines and summaries in 200+ languages, making it easier for non-English speakers and newcomers to access the news that shapes their lives.

Why now? In moments when information moves fast, good decisions depend on good reporting. Our team believes technology should elevate reliable journalism—not drown it out. Newsly applies smart filtering to surface balanced coverage and reduce repetition, so you get signal, not noise.

What’s next? Starting today, Newsly is available on both iOS and Android. Over the coming months we’ll roll out personalized topic packs, deeper local coverage, and more control over notifications. We’re listening closely, so if you have feedback or feature requests, we’d love to hear from you.

Ready to try it? Download Newsly now using the links above and experience a calmer way to stay informed. Whether you skim the Morning Brief, listen hands-free, or browse in your native language, we hope Newsly helps you feel more informed—and less overwhelmed.

Thank you for being here at launch. This is just the beginning.
